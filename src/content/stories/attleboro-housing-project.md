A developer is asking Attleboro's planning board to approve a mixed-use building a few blocks from the downtown commuter rail station.

The proposal includes apartments on the upper floors and retail space at street level. It falls within the area the city rezoned to comply with the state's MBTA Communities law, which requires multifamily housing to be allowed by right near transit.

Abutters at the first hearing raised concerns about parking and traffic on nearby side streets. The board continued the hearing to its next meeting.
