Fall is peak season for the county's farms. Here's where to shop this month.

## SouthCoast

**Dartmouth:** Weekend markets run through October with produce, flowers and local seafood.

**Westport:** Farm stands along the back roads are open daily. Bring cash for the honor-box stands.

## North county

**Rehoboth and Seekonk:** Pick-your-own apple orchards are open on weekends. Check each farm's social media before you go, since picking conditions change with the weather.
