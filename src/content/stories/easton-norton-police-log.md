Police in Easton and Norton are investigating a string of catalytic converter thefts from vehicles parked in driveways and business lots over the past two weeks.

Police in both towns are asking anyone with home security footage from overnight hours to contact the detective division. They recommend parking in a garage or well-lit area when possible.
