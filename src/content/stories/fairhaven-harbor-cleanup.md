Volunteers fanned out along the Fairhaven and Acushnet shoreline Saturday morning for the annual coastal cleanup, filling dumpsters with rope, buoys, bottles and one shopping cart.

Organizers said turnout was up from last year. Much of the debris was fishing gear, which organizers plan to send to a recycling program instead of a landfill.
