Fall River's school committee spent most of Tuesday's meeting on a question the district has put off for years: repair its oldest middle school, or replace it.

Consultants presented two options. A renovation would keep students in the building during construction; a new school would take longer but last decades more. Both hinge on how much the Massachusetts School Building Authority agrees to cover.

## By the numbers

- Two options on the table: renovate or replace.
- The district's share depends on the state reimbursement rate.
- A local funding vote would be needed either way.

The committee plans a community forum before choosing a preferred option this fall.
