The Freetown-Fall River State Forest covers more than 5,000 acres, and fall is the best time to explore it. Here are five routes for different ability levels.

1. **An easy loop** from the main parking area, good for kids and dogs.
2. **A longer ridge walk** with views over the tree canopy.
3. **A pond route** that's quiet on weekday mornings.
4. **A mountain bike–friendly trail** that hikers share on weekends.
5. **A historic route** past old cellar holes and stone walls.

Wear blaze orange during hunting season and check the state's trail conditions page before heading out.
