Mansfield's fall town meeting is scheduled for next month, and the warrant is out. Here's a quick look at the articles likely to draw debate.

## Key articles

1. **Capital spending:** Funding requests for public works vehicles and school building repairs.
2. **Zoning update:** Changes to accessory dwelling unit rules to line up with state law.
3. **Stabilization fund:** A new fund to set aside money for future capital needs.

Any registered voter can attend and vote. The full warrant is available at the town clerk's office and on the town website.
