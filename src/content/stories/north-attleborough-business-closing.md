A family-owned hardware store that has served North Attleborough for three generations will close at the end of December, its owners announced this week.

The owners said no single factor drove the decision. They cited retirement, competition from big-box stores and the difficulty of finding someone to take over. A closing sale starts next month.

The building is listed for sale. Town officials said they have not received any permit applications for the site.
