Families in the region got their first detailed look at next year's school schedule this week, including later start times for older students.

District officials said the change follows research on adolescent sleep. Elementary schools will shift earlier to keep the number of bus routes the same.

## What changes

- Middle and high school start times move later.
- Elementary start times move earlier.
- After-school programs will adjust pickup times to match.

A family information night is planned for next month.
