Drivers on Route 6 should expect overnight lane closures near the Swansea–Somerset line through November as crews repair the bridge approach.

Work is scheduled from 8 p.m. to 5 a.m. on weeknights. Police details will direct traffic, and one lane will stay open in each direction.

State highway officials recommend allowing extra time or using Route 138 as an alternate.
