A long-vacant storefront facing Taunton Green is getting a new tenant. A bakery-café plans to open in October, according to signs posted in the window and a permit application filed with the city.

The owners told The Bristol Brief they'll focus on morning pastries, including Portuguese sweet bread and malasadas, alongside espresso drinks and a handful of sandwiches at lunch.

## The details

- **Where:** Taunton Green, downtown Taunton
- **When:** Targeting mid-October
- **Hours:** Early morning through mid-afternoon, closed Mondays
